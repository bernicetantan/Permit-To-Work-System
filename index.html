Permit to Work — 3-Stage Approval Process
  • Stage 1 — You (Permit Applicant): complete this form accurately and sign to submit — this counts as your Stage 1 endorsement
  • Stage 2 — Specialist: physically visits the work site to verify all preconditions are met before issuing the permit
  • Stage 3 — Site / Operations Manager: reviews for concurrent work conflicts and gives final authorisation
  • Work must NOT begin until Stages 2 and 3 are both signed — a submitted permit is not an authorised permit
  • The approved permit must be displayed at the work area and closed out on completion
